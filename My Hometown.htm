Kabul: The Capital of Afghanistan

History

Kabul is the capital and one of the largest cities of Afghanistan.It has a long history and has been an important center of trade and culture for many years. Many people from different parts of Afghanistan live in Kabul

Places to Visit

Kabul has many interesting places to visit. Babur Gardens is a beautiful historic garden. The National Museum of Afghanistan is another important place where visitors can learn about the country's history and culture. People can also visit other parks and markets in the city

Famous Food

Kabul is known for delicious Afghan food. Kabuli Pulao is one of the most famous dishes. It is made with rie,carrots,raisins, and meat. many people also enjoy mantu and ashak, which are popular Afghan dishes
